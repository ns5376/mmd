Extract ONLY the FIRST LINE (أولها / incipit) of each manuscript text in Arabic.

What to look for:
- The catalogue usually labels this with words like:
  - "أولها:" , "أوله:" , "أوله" , "أولها"
- It is the first words of the text itself, not of the catalogue description.
- It often comes near the end of each entry, after the physical description (pages, dimensions, condition, etc.).

Guidelines:
- For each title listed for this page, extract ONE and ONLY ONE first line in Arabic.
- You will be given the titles on this page in order. Return the first lines in the same order, one per title.
- If a title clearly has no first line given in the catalogue, return an empty string "" in that position and keep the order.
- The first line should be short: only the very beginning of the text (the first phrase or sentence), not a long excerpt.
- Preserve the exact wording and order as written (including diacritics and punctuation if present).
- Do NOT translate; keep it in Arabic script only.
- Do NOT confuse the incipit with the title or with other quotations in the description.

Examples of first lines:
- "الحمد لله الذي جعل في الكتاب مشفاة للأسقام"
- "أما بعد حمد الله والصلاة على نبيه فإن هذا كتاب ..."
